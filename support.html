Contact

Email afterwordr@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two business days. For a bug, please include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, the app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings tab) and, if you can, the file that caused the trouble.

Adding books

  • From Files or iCloud Drive: tap + in the Library and pick one or more files. You can also open a file from Mail, Safari or any app with "Open in" and choose Afterword.
  • Send to Afterword folder: drop files into Files > On My iPhone (or iPad) > Afterword > Send to Afterword. They import the next time the app is open.
  • From a book server: Browse > Add Catalog and enter the address of your OPDS catalog (Calibre-Web, Kavita, Komga or any OPDS feed). Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks are built in.
  • Audiobooks: add M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC or FLAC files the same way, or sign in to an Audiobookshelf server under Browse.

Supported formats: EPUB, PDF, CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT comics, and DRM-free MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PRC, FB2, TXT, Markdown and HTML, which are converted to EPUB when you add them.

Questions

Can Afterword open my Kindle or Audible purchases?

No. Afterword reads DRM-free files only. Books and audiobooks protected by DRM (most Kindle store purchases, all of Audible) cannot be opened, and Afterword does not remove DRM. Kindle highlights and notes can still be imported from a My Clippings file or a notebook export.

How do I import my highlights?

Settings > Import Highlights and Lists. Afterword reads Kindle "My Clippings.txt" (from the Kindle's documents folder) and notebook HTML exports from the Kindle app, Apple Books annotation databases, and Goodreads or StoryGraph CSV exports. Highlights for books already in your library are placed in the text; the rest wait under "Not in Your Library" in the Notebook until you add the book.

Why don't I see word hints or Smart Reading?

Those features use Apple's on-device model, so they need iOS 26 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other devices, hints appear only for words saved in your Vocabulary, and Smart Reading is hidden.

Audiobook alignment takes a long time

Lining an audiobook up with its ebook uses on-device speech recognition, so it needs the book's language to be supported for on-device recognition and the app to stay open. A long audiobook can take a while; if you leave, it picks up where it stopped.

What syncs over iCloud?

Library details, reading positions, highlights and notes, reading sessions and settings, plus your book files if you turn on "Sync Book Files". Per-book text settings, drawings on PDFs and audiobook alignments stay on the device where you made them. Sync needs the same Apple Account on each device; you can turn it off in Settings > iCloud.

Does Afterword send my books anywhere?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and the developer runs no servers. Data leaves your device only for services you set up yourself (your own book server, KOReader sync, Hardcover, Readwise) or if you choose to turn on a cloud AI provider, which is off by default. See the privacy policy.

A book will not open or looks wrong

Try the file in another reader to confirm it is not damaged, then email it to the address above with a note about what you saw. Files that are not DRM-free, or that are protected archives, cannot be opened.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. What is in iCloud belongs to your Apple Account: turn off sync in Afterword's Settings, then remove Afterword's data under iOS Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
